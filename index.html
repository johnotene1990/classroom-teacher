How to use the classroom

  1. Select a number from 1 to 6.
  2. Click Point at number to make the stickman point.
  3. Click Circle number to draw a circle.
  4. Click Underline to underline the number.
  5. Click Explain number to explain the number.
  6. Click Wave hand to greet the class.
  7. Use Erase marking or Clear board to clean the board.
